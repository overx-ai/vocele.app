# 000 - Blog Strategy

## Vision
Own the long-tail search + AI-answer-engine queries around **speaking** (not
studying) a language — the exact positioning of the product. Every article is a
working answer first and a funnel second. Articles are **original per locale,
never translations**: each page is self-canonical with no hreflang twin, so each
locale targets its own keywords with its own content.

## Target Audience
| Segment | Pain Points | Content Needs |
|---------|-------------|---------------|
| es-MX: US Spanish-speaking immigrants ("María, 34, Houston") | Shame when speaking; frozen at interviews, school meetings, DMV; understands but can't speak | Confidence path, pronunciation fixes for Spanish speakers, cost-honest comparisons in dollars |
| en-US: adult learners incl. same avatar in EN-display mode | Accent friction, plateaued fluency, tutor costs | Accent mechanics, AI-vs-tutor decision content, exam (IELTS/TOEFL) speaking prep |
