Privacy policy

Effective: 8 October 2026 · Last updated: 19 October 2026

The short version

  • You sign in with Apple or Google. We keep what Vocele needs to teach you: your account, your learning profile, your progress, and what you say and write in lessons.
  • When you speak, the recording goes to our speech providers to be transcribed and scored. We keep the transcript and the score, not the recording, unless you choose to share a take with other learners.
  • AI providers process your content to run the coach. We do not use your content to train AI models.
  • Only if you turn it on in Settings → My Data, AppsFlyer measures which ad brought you to Vocele, and it reads your advertising identifier only if you also allow tracking when iOS asks. We do not sell personal information for money.
  • This website uses Google Analytics only after you accept cookies.
  • You can delete your account in the app, see and delete what the coach remembers about you, and ask us for a copy of your data.

  • Customer Support (App Functionality): messages you send us (Section 13)
  • Crash Data (App Functionality): crash reports (Section 14)
  • Performance Data (App Functionality): how fast the app works for you (Section 14)
  • Device ID (Third-Party Advertising): the advertising identifier above (Section 15)

03What we do not collect

  • No password: you sign in only with Apple or Google.
  • No phone number, contacts, photos or health data from your iPhone.
  • No precise location: no GPS. We work out only your country, from your IP address (Section 5).
  • No payment card or bank details: Apple handles every payment (Section 12).
  • No advertising inside the app.

04Your account

You need an account to use Vocele, and you create it by signing in with Apple or with Google. There is no email-and-password sign-in and no guest account. When you sign in we receive and store:

  • your email address. With Sign in with Apple you can hide your real address, and then we only receive a private relay address from Apple;
  • your name and profile picture, if Apple or Google shares them with us;
  • an account identifier, which is how we keep your learning data together;
  • an identifier for your device and the device's name, to keep your sessions secure.

We use these to run your account, to sign you in on your devices and to contact you about the service. We do not send marketing email. Signing in is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy or Google's Privacy Policy, whichever you use.

05Your learning profile

To build your lessons we keep the languages you learn and the language the app talks to you in, your level in each language, your time zone and the times you want reminders.

When you set the app up you can also tell us your age range, your goals, how you heard about Vocele and how you like to learn. Every one of these questions can be skipped. We use the answers to shape your daily plan and how the coach talks to you, and in total to understand who Vocele helps.

We work out your country from your IP address. It is not your precise location. If you join a leaderboard, your country is shown there as a flag (Section 11).

06Your voice

Vocele listens only while you are doing something spoken that you started: a lesson, a chat with the coach, a pronunciation drill or a duel. Your recording is sent to our speech providers so that what you said can be transcribed and scored.

  • Recordings are not kept after they have been processed.
  • The transcript and the scores are kept with your progress, for up to 400 days.
  • On your iPhone, your recent takes are kept so you can listen back. You can delete them, and they are removed when you sign out or delete your account.
  • Some short answers are recognised by Apple's speech recognition, on your iPhone where it supports that; otherwise iOS sends that recording to Apple, under Apple's privacy policy.

Two features keep a recording on purpose, and only if you opt in:

  • Arena duels. If you take part, your duel takes can be played to other learners as their opponent. They are kept for 30 days after you last refreshed them, or until you withdraw your consent.
  • Week's Best. If you enter, your showcase take can be played to other learners of that language. It is deleted 30 days after its week ends, or when you withdraw.

Other learners hear these takes under a generated handle, never your name.

07What you write, and your conversations with the coach

We keep your chat messages, the answers you type, the words you save and the translations you ask for, so your history is there next time and the coach can correct you. The messages in a conversation are kept for up to 180 days after it ends; its topic and the short summary the coach writes of it stay while your account exists. Words you save stay in your vocabulary while your account exists.

The coach remembers some facts about you between conversations, such as your job or a trip you are planning, so lessons can be about your life. You can see these AI memories, and delete any of them or all of them, in Settings → My Data.

08How AI is used on your content

The coach's replies, corrections, feedback, translations and spoken audio are produced by AI. To do that, what you write and say is processed by AI providers on our behalf (Section 18). They receive what each request needs and nothing more:

  • Your voice recordings go to speech-recognition and pronunciation-assessment providers, to transcribe and score what you said.
  • Your chat messages and typed answers go to AI language-model providers, to write the coach's replies, corrections and translations.
  • The coach's replies and other text the app reads aloud go to text-to-speech providers, to produce the spoken audio.

The app asks you first. Before any of your recordings or messages is sent to these providers, the app asks whether you agree. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → My Data → AI processing. If you say no, the features that need these providers, such as talking with the coach and pronunciation scores, are switched off and show you how to turn them back on; everything else in the app keeps working.

We do not use your content to train AI models.

09Automated decisions and safety

Automated systems help us find abuse and harassment, and signs that someone may be at risk. They may limit what an account can do or suspend it, and they may show you crisis resources.

If an automated decision affects you, you can contest it: write to support@overx.ai and a person will review it. You can also give your point of view and ask us to explain the decision. This is your right under Article 22 of the GDPR and the UK GDPR, and we give it to everyone.

10Your progress and learning history

We keep your vocabulary and its review schedule, the exercises you do, your streaks and achievements, and how many minutes you have spoken and how much of your plan you have used. This is what your daily plan, statistics and allowance are built from.

It is kept while your account exists. Your exercise history is kept for up to 400 days.

11What other people can see

Nothing you do in Vocele is public unless you choose to make it so.

  • Leaderboards, the public duel board, the Arena and Week's Best, if you opt in, show a handle the app generates for you, your score, your country flag and, for the Arena and Week's Best, your takes (Section 6).
  • Friends you add see your activity, such as wins and level-ups, and your challenge ratings.
  • Roadmap ideas you submit may be shown publicly after we review them.
  • Anything you share yourself, such as an invite link, a share card or a clip, goes wherever you send it.

12Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and paid for through Apple's App Store. We never see your card or bank details. We receive the status of your purchase and subscription (which plan, when it renews or lapses, and whether it was refunded), linked to your account, through our subscription and purchase management provider. That is how the app knows what your plan includes.

13Support and feedback

When you write to us through the in-app support chat or the feedback form, we receive your message, your email address if you give it, and your device model, iOS version and app version, so we can answer and fix what you report. A customer-support messaging provider handles these messages for us. We keep them for up to 24 months.

14Diagnostics and analytics

The app sends us crash reports, performance measurements and usage events, linked to your account. We use them to keep Vocele working, to find what is slow or broken and to understand, in total, which parts help people learn. They never contain the text of what you said or wrote. We keep them for up to 13 months.

Our own analytics do not use your advertising identifier.

15Advertising attribution

We want to know which ads and links bring people to Vocele, so we spend money where it reaches people who want to learn a language. This is measurement, and Vocele itself shows no ads.

  • Apple. If you found Vocele through an ad on the App Store, Apple can tell us which campaign led to the install, without telling us who you are.
  • AppsFlyer. AppsFlyer matches an install to the ad or link that led to it. It starts only after you turn on attribution in Settings → My Data, wherever you live. It then receives information about your device and the install, such as the device model, iOS version, app version, your IP address and when the app was installed and opened. It reads Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you also choose Allow when iOS asks whether Vocele may track you. Using the advertising identifier this way is tracking as Apple defines it, and the App Store lists it so. AppsFlyer's Privacy Policy.

Under California law, giving AppsFlyer this information can count as "sharing" personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising (Section 25). To stop it, turn attribution off in Settings → My Data, turn off tracking for Vocele in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, or use AppsFlyer's opt-out page. Saying no changes nothing else in the app.

16This website and cookies

If you agree, this website uses Google Analytics, from Google, to count visits and see which pages help. It measures the pages you visit, how you use the site, your device and browser type, and your approximate location worked out from your IP address. Google receives your IP address and details of your browser and device. We have turned off Google's advertising features, so it is not used for advertising. How Google uses data from sites that use its services.

Nothing is measured until you choose Accept in the cookie banner. If you choose Reject, nothing loads. If your browser sends the Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as Reject and do not show the banner. To change your choice or withdraw your consent, choose Cookie settings at the bottom of any page; withdrawing removes the analytics cookies.

Cookies this website sets:

  • _ga and _ga_0ZQBJJGWPG: Google Analytics, only after you accept, to tell visits apart. They last up to 14 months, and we keep the analytics for no longer than 14 months.
  • vocele_consent: your answer to the cookie banner, for about 6 months.

Our hosting provider receives your IP address and basic details of each visit to deliver the page and protect the site.

21Deleting your account, and getting a copy

You can delete your account in the app at any time: Settings → Account → Delete account. This erases your account and your sign-in record, your learning profile, progress, saved words, conversations, AI memories, transcripts, scores and any takes you shared, and removes your data from your iPhone. It also revokes Vocele's access to your Apple sign-in, and we ask our attribution and subscription-management providers to delete their records about you. It cannot be undone. Deleting your account does not cancel an Apple subscription: cancel that in your Apple Account settings.

To get a copy of your data, write to support@overx.ai. We send it in a machine-readable format within 30 days.

22Security

Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and access to it is limited to the people who need it to run Vocele. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data and is likely to put you at risk, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

23Children

Vocele is not directed at children under 13. In the EEA and the UK it is not for anyone under 16 unless a parent or guardian consents, or the law of their country sets a lower age. If we learn that we have collected data from a child without that, we delete it. If you think a child has given us personal data, write to support@overx.ai.

24Your rights

Under the GDPR and the UK GDPR, and in the same way wherever you live, you have the right to:

  • access your personal data and get a copy of it;
  • correct it if it is wrong;
  • delete it;
  • restrict how we use it while a question about it is settled;
  • receive the data you gave us in a portable, machine-readable form;
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interests;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting what happened before;
  • contest an automated decision and have a person review it (Section 9);
  • complain to a data protection authority.

Brazil's LGPD and similar laws elsewhere give you comparable rights, and we honour them the same way.

To use a right, write to support@overx.ai. We may ask for something that confirms the request is yours, so we never hand your data to someone else, and we answer within 30 days. If a request is complex we may need up to two more months, and we will tell you why. We will not treat you differently for using a right.

We are supervised by Georgia's Personal Data Protection Service. If you are in the EU, the EEA or the UK, you can also complain to the data protection authority where you live or work. We would rather you came to us first.

26Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

This website honours the Global Privacy Control signal: if your browser sends it, we treat it as Reject for cookies and as an opt-out of sharing, and do not show the cookie banner. There is no agreed standard for the older Do Not Track signal, so we do not respond to it separately; the website loads no analytics without your consent either way.
